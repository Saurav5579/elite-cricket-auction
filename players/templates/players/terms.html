Terms, Refund & Privacy Policy

Welcome to the Elite Cricket Championship Auction Platform. By accessing or using this website, you agree to comply with the following policies regarding player registration, auction participation, payments, and data privacy.

🏏 1. Player Registration

Players must provide accurate personal and cricket-related information during registration. Any false information may lead to disqualification.

⚡ 2. Auction Participation

Only verified players will participate in the auction. Organizers have the right to approve or reject any registration.

💳 3. Payment Policy

Registration fees once paid are non-refundable unless the tournament is cancelled by the organizers.

🤝 4. Player Conduct

Players must maintain professional behavior during auction and tournament activities.

📊 5. Team Selection

Teams will bid for players according to official auction rules.

💰 Refund Policy

All payments made for player registration are final and non-refundable except if the tournament is cancelled.

If a player withdraws after registration, the registration fee will not be refunded.

🔐 Privacy Policy

Elite Cricket Championship respects user privacy. Personal data collected during registration will only be used for tournament management and communication.

We do not sell or share user data with third parties except for payment processing.

All payment transactions are securely processed through Razorpay.

📩 Contact

For questions regarding these policies please contact the Elite Cricket Championship organizing team.
